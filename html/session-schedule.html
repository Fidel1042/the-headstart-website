The 6 features of a strong candidate

To build the foundation, a mentee must have at least 3 out of 6. Based on conversations with 500+ students and grads, and 30+ offers secured across tech, business and engineering.

1. Relevant internship experience

  • Local internship preferred. One from their home country also works, as it builds employer confidence they can do the job.
  • This is the "juice" to a resume or LinkedIn
  • Virtual internships (Forage and similar) do not count as valid internship experience to most hirers

2. Society executive role

  • Executive means leading a function of the society, sometimes managing sub-committees
  • Gives them real world experience distinct from internships

3. 85+ WAM, relevant professional certificates, or wins in case competitions

  • Grades matter only above 85+
  • A relevant, widely recognised certificate makes a difference. CFA for finance, CISSP for cyber security.
  • Case competition wins count if top 3 or similarly notable

4. Relevant passion project

  • Showcases proactivity and interest in the field
  • Must have direct impact on their daily life or social circle, preferably quantifiable
  • AI can help execute the project, but direct impact is non-negotiable

5. Unique personal story / fun facts

  • Everyone has unique stories
  • Unique stories make them memorable among hundreds of applicants
  • 1 to 2 relevant fun facts can help them stand out immediately

6. Australia job experience, including casual roles and volunteering

  • Local experience is king, it shows they can communicate with people in Australia
  • Casual or part-time work reassures employers on communication skills
  • No job? Volunteering works too, if done consistently, for example once every 2 weeks

Unique personal story / fun facts

Fun fact: they already have them. They just need a third opinion to validate and point out these unique stories. Here is the step by step to extract them.

  1. Write down every single memorable activity you have done since high school to today
  2. Write down your target field (marketing, accounting, software engineer)
  3. List out 2 memorable incidents per activity that relate to your target field
  4. Find a person already inside the industry, or who has had an internship at least
  5. Present your timeline to them and ask them to highlight what is interesting about you. If you cannot find that person, we can help you.
Time required2 to 4 hours
Use it inLinkedIn About section, resume professional profile, interview answers

Relevant passion project

A passion project should not be generic AI slop. These avenues work across every field:

  • Non-assignment related academic research
  • Technical tools that improve your life or the life of your social circle, for example a finance tracker
  • Blogs or articles posted regularly
  • Business ideas to make money. It has to relate to your field, or to a part of business that uses your particular skills.

Key to a successful passion project

  1. It must generate an impact or result. For example: saved $x, got x users, got x likes on a social media post, made $x, got recognition from a professor.
  2. You must have put in 20+ hours combined. When you work hard towards a project you gain genuine confidence in your own work, and that confidence is the difference maker during interviews. We have talked to 600+ mentees, recruiters and managers, and everyone can tell whether a passion project is AI slop or the real deal.
Time required3 to 4 weeks
Use it inLinkedIn and resume experience, interview answers

Three ways to get one, easiest to hardest:

  1. Text every single friend of yours in Australia and ask if their part time workplace has any vacancies
  2. Online applications
  3. Print a resume of your past casual job history, go to the suburbs near you, walk into every shop, introduce yourself and ask if you can hand a resume to the manager

Volunteering

To find volunteering activities that are available, give this prompt to your LLM of choice:

Context: I live in [insert suburb and state]. I'm building a candidacy for [insert field].

Task: Find 5 volunteer opportunities that could strengthen this candidacy.

Criteria (all must apply):
- In or related to [insert field]
- Involves teamwork: regular interaction and collaboration with other volunteers, not solo work
- Easy to get accepted (minimal vetting or interview process)
- Can start within 1 month from today
- Ongoing, regular commitment (not a one-off event)

Output format per option:
- Name + hyperlink
- 1 to 2 sentence description
- Acceptance difficulty rating (Easy / Medium / Hard, with a one-line reason why)
