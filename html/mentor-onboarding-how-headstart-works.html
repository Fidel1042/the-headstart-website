How The Headstart Works

Your resources

Everything you need sits in the mentor portal under Resources. Have a read of all three before your first session.

01 · Mentor Resource Hub

The reference library. Resume and LinkedIn feedback, cover letters, where to find roles, the networking playbook, coffee chat etiquette, interview questions, screening calls, psychometric tests, assessment centres and case studies.

Use it when you are not sure how to advise on a specific part of the job search. Pull the section you need and adapt it, it is a reference and not a script.

Open the Resource Hub →

02 · Accountability shared document

A shared tracker you copy once per mentee. Use it to keep you and the mentee's progress on the same page, so nobody is guessing what was agreed last session or what is still outstanding.

Open the tracker →

03 · Recommended Structure Flow for Weekly Sessions

A recommended 12 to 15 session structure, so you never run out of ideas on what to cover next. It runs from goal setting and materials, through outreach and coffee chats, into screening calls, story banks and mock interviews, and finishes on live interview prep.

You do not have to follow it strictly. We are more than happy if you run your own weekly structure, and if a mentee has an ad hoc request that takes priority.

Open the structure flow →

04 · Difficult Mentees?

How to deal with mentees, at a high level. The two broad types you will meet, the specific personalities that need a different approach, and when to flag something to Fidel.

Worth reading early. Most of the difficulty in mentoring is not the job search advice, it is working out what this particular person actually needs from you.

Open Difficult Mentees →

At the end of each session, please tick all of the following

  1. Set at least 1 take home task
  2. Mention what will be in the next session
  3. Confirm a next session date in the call
  • If the mentee has more important requests, always listen and be flexible on the plan.
  • If a mentee consistently fails to do their tasks, check Difficult Mentees for how to deal with it.

Module Checklist

  • ✓ I have read the Mentor Resource Hub and know what is in it
  • ✓ I have read the recommended structure flow and understand the 12 to 15 session shape
  • ✓ I have read Difficult Mentees and know how to handle the different personalities
  • ✓ I understand how mentees are matched with their mentor
  • ✓ I know that Fidel manages the WhatsApp group and is the main point of contact
  • ✓ I will set a task, flag what is next, and confirm the next session date at the end of every session
