Our Mentoring Philosophy

What Headstart Mentoring is about

Other than being their mentor, we are also their job search buddy. We do more than just being transactional.

Here is what we value in a good mentor:

  • Likeliness. Whether we can get along and have a good time with the mentee.
  • Continuity. Whether we can get the mentee to do regular, weekly sessions.
  • Value. Whether we can give enough value on the job search, catered to the mentee.
  • Flexibility. Whether we are flexible on our mentoring style according to their needs.

Boundaries

  • Keep everything on the platform. Taking mentees privately or being paid outside it means we can't keep working together, so if a mentee ever suggests it, let Fidel or Koko know.
  • Advice belongs in sessions. If a mentee messages you between sessions, point them to booking one.
  • Anything you are unsure about, ask us first. We would much rather talk it through than have you guess.

  • If a client specifically requests you, you will be prioritised for matching.
