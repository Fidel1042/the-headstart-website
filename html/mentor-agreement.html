The Headstart · Mentor Contractor Agreement · Confidential

1. Background

A. We consider you qualified to mentor for The Headstart.

B. You agree to provide them on these terms.

C. This Agreement supersedes any prior understanding on its subject matter.

D. Any unenforceable provision is severed and the rest continues.

2. Capacity / independent contractor

2.1 You act as an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent.

2.2 Nothing here creates employment or partnership.

2.3 You are responsible for your own tax, superannuation, and insurance.

2.4 You may not bind or represent us unless authorised in writing.

3. Position

3.1 You are engaged as a Mentor, or another role agreed in writing.

3.2 You work independently and, where required, with our representatives.

4. Commencement

This Agreement commences on the date in Item 1 of the Schedule.

5. Responsibilities

5.1 Your responsibilities are in Item 2 and may be varied by us.

5.2 You must:

  • (a) promote our interests;
  • (b) protect our reputation;
  • (c) avoid conflicts with our interests;
  • (d) act honestly, diligently, and professionally; and
  • (e) work to the best of your ability.

5.3 We may direct you to perform, or stop performing, any duty at any time.

6. Method and frequency of payment

6.1 You are paid at the rate in Item 3.

6.2 You must invoice through our process. We may provide a template.

6.3 Payment is weekly in arrears.

6.4 Nothing is owed for services not delivered or not approved by us.

7. Expenses

7.1 You bear all expenses of providing the services.

7.2 Nothing is reimbursed unless approved by us in writing beforehand.

8. Term and termination

8.1 This Agreement continues until terminated.

8.2 Either party may terminate on 14 days’ written notice.

8.3 We may terminate immediately if you:

  • (a) breach a material provision;
  • (b) breach confidentiality;
  • (c) engage in misconduct or damage our reputation;
  • (d) solicit our clients or students; or
  • (e) otherwise act against our interests.

8.4 Confidentiality, intellectual property, Resource Packs, and non-solicitation survive termination.

9. Our property

9.1 Our property remains ours at all times.

9.2 It includes Confidential Information, Intellectual Property, Resource Packs, records, and data in any form.

9.3 On request or termination, you must return or delete it.

10. Confidential information

10.1 You must keep confidential all information about us, our students, mentors, systems, pricing, and business affairs (“Confidential Information”).

10.2 You may use or disclose it only to perform the services, or where required by law.

10.3 Breach is serious misconduct and grounds for immediate termination.

10.4 This clause continues indefinitely after termination.

11. Intellectual property

11.1 All Intellectual Property you create in the course of your services is ours.

11.2 This applies whether created alone or jointly, in or out of hours.

11.3 You must disclose it to us immediately.

11.4 You assign all rights in it to us and must do what is needed to perfect ownership.

11.5 You must not use or distribute it without our written consent.

11.6 This clause continues after termination.

12. Resource Packs and materials

12.1 “Resource Packs” means any guides, templates, decks, worksheets, session plans, video, audio, or other resources produced, curated, or branded by us for the mentoring services, in any format, complete or in development.

12.2 We may make Resource Packs available for your sessions, and you must use them as we direct.

12.3 Resource Packs are Confidential Information and remain ours. Clauses 10 and 11 apply in full.

12.4 You must not:

  • (a) copy, share, publish, sell, or sublicense a Resource Pack to anyone other than students engaged through us;
  • (b) use one outside this Agreement; or
  • (c) rebrand or present one as your own work.

12.5 Anything you contribute to a Resource Pack forms part of it and is ours under Clause 11.

12.6 On request or termination, you must return or delete all Resource Packs and any copies or extracts.

12.7 This clause continues indefinitely after termination.

13. Non-solicitation

13.1 During this Agreement and for 12 months after termination, you must not directly or indirectly:

  • (a) solicit any student or client of ours;
  • (b) provide services outside this Agreement to anyone introduced to you through us; or
  • (c) induce any contractor or mentor of ours to leave.

14. Non-competition

14.1 During this Agreement and for 6 months after termination, you must not, within Australia, provide career mentoring, coaching, resume, interview, or job application services that compete with us, on your own account or for anyone else.

14.2 Clause 14.1 does not restrict your primary occupation or employment outside career mentoring.

14.3 The restraint is reasonable and necessary to protect our Confidential Information, Resource Packs, and student relationships.

14.4 If clause 14.1 is unenforceable, it is read down as far as needed, and if it cannot be, it is severed without affecting the rest.

15. General

15.1 This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

15.2 You may not transfer your obligations without our written consent.

15.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Australia and, where applicable, New South Wales.

15.4 Each party will do what is needed to give effect to this Agreement.

16. Marketing & publicity consent

16.1 You consent to us using your name, photo, professional background, and career information for marketing, including on our website and social media.

16.2 Such use is limited to representing you as a Mentor and will not disclose sensitive personal information.

16.3 You may withdraw consent in writing. It applies to future publications only.

Schedule

Item 1 – Commencement date. The date you sign below.

Item 2 – Role description. Mentor, providing 1:1 sessions to our students using our Resource Packs as directed, and contributing to Resource Packs where requested.
