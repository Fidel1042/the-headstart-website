# Mentor Portal App: Rollout Plan

Goal: every mentor has the portal on their home screen and logs sessions from their phone within a minute of finishing a session. Faster logging means cleaner billing data and fewer chased-up mentors.

## Phase 0: You and Koko (day 1)

Both of you install it and run through the full TESTING-GUIDE.md checklist. Do not move to Phase 1 until everything passes on at least one iPhone and one Android.

## Phase 1: Pilot with 2 mentors (days 2 to 4)

Pick two mentors: one iPhone user and one Android user, ideally your most responsive ones. Message them individually (not in the group) with the install message below.

After 2 days, ask them these three questions:

1. Did the install work first try? If not, where did it get stuck?
2. Have you logged a session from the app yet? Was anything annoying?
3. Anything missing that would make you open it more?

Fix anything they raise before Phase 2.

## Phase 2: Everyone (day 5 onwards)

Send the message below to the mentor WhatsApp group. Pin it.

### Ready-to-send WhatsApp message

> Hey team! The mentor portal is now an app you can put on your home screen. Takes under a minute:
>
> iPhone: open theheadstartmentoring.com/mentor-portal in Safari, log in, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen"
>
> Android: open the same link in Chrome, log in, tap the three dots, then "Install app"
>
> You'll get the gold TH icon and it opens like a normal app. Log your session right after you finish it, takes 20 seconds. Everything else you need (mentee info, resources, your session history) is in there too.
>
> Reply here once you've added it so I can tick you off, and tell me if anything looks weird on your phone.
