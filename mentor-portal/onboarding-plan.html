Onboarding plan

The order a new mentor goes through, from the doc to a full mentor. Steps 4 and 5 are a probation gate and are internal only.

1

Onboarding doc

They read it before anything else happens.

2

Onboarding call

45 minutes, covering expectations, values, how they handle a mentee who does not do the work, and their lesson plan.

3

Shadow sessions · must have

Not optional. They sit in before they run anything of their own.

4

Another shadow session, if their continuity rate says so

Depends on the continuity rate. Definition still to be set.

5

Fidel sits in on a session

Once they have done 3 sessions with one mentee, or 5 sessions in total, whichever comes first.

Probation · internal only

Steps 4 and 5 are the gate. A mentor stays on if they prove their continuity. Never described to mentors in these terms.

6

Feedback check-in call

After the sit-in. What was seen, what changes.

Steps 3 and 5 are tracked on the Delivery check-in page, which lists every mentor who has hit the 3-with-one or 5-in-total mark.
