Mentor pipeline

Everyone being screened or interviewed. Move someone along, set their interview time, and set their rate, all from here. Once they are Hired they move to Agreements.

Interview questions
  1. Introduction
  2. How did you get this role?
  3. Why do you want to be a Mentor at the Headstart?
  4. What steps would you take to understand their needs and their working style in the first session
  5. Do you have 1 trick that you used that helped you stand out from other candidates yourself?
  6. Are you comfortable mentoring mentees not going into your industry exactly?
  7. On a high level, how would you structure weekly sessions with the mentee that lasts around 12-15 sessions?
  8. If you take a mentee, the expectation is you do 1 session per week with them, how many hours do you have per week for that?
  9. Tell them about the rate - $20 to start
