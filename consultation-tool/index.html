Sales call · 6 stages · 6 questions, test only if you need it

1

Open

Say

"Hey [name], how are you?"

"I'll ask you a few things so I can work out where you're at and match you with the right mentor. Sound good?"

2

Diagnose

They talk, you listen. Do not pitch.

1 · Industry

"What industry are you trying to get into?"

2 · Why they're here

"Why did you sign up to this call?"

Let them say it in their own words before you frame anything.

3 · Help so far

"What help have you had so far?"

"What were the results?"

4 · Why it didn't work

"Why do you think it didn't work?"

"Why do you think you still haven't landed anything?"

5 · Timeline

"When do you need this job by?"

"Where are you at with study and your visa?"

6 · Barrier Optional

"What do you think is your biggest barrier?"

If they say they don't know, go to the test.

7 · Type of mentoring

"What kind of mentoring do you need? Hands-on and tactical, or advice-based?"

If they say "same industry" or "someone who was international"

"That's the bare minimum, that's what the guarantee covers. I'm asking beyond that."

If they fish for tactics, "what did you do to get your offers?"

"I'll show you exactly, but first I want to get your full picture."

After each question, go quiet. Let them fill the silence.

3
Only if you need it

The test

Pull it when
  • They don't know their barrier
  • They blame the market or not applying enough
  • They call it a small fix
  • They go soft at the close
Leave it when
  • They named a real barrier
  • They're asking how soon they can start
  • They're already sold
Say

"Can I try something quick? Two minutes, and it tells me more than anything else I could ask."

"I'll give you a minute to prep. Then answer it like it's the real thing: tell me a little bit about yourself."

Give the real minute. Stay silent through it.

Then, no feedback

"Okay. I understand where you're at."

Move straight on. No pause.

If they ask for feedback on their answer

"That's exactly what your mentor takes you through. I just needed to see where you're starting from."

Never coach here. Never run this on someone already sold.

4

Pitch, when asked

Hand over: "That's everything from me. What have you got for me?"

If they ask "how does this work?" open Session structure

Name their quadrant, walk the stages, and name the one that gets the weight because it's their gap. "For you we camp on [stage]." Then: "Does that path make sense?"

Never say you skip a stage. Weight, not removal.

If they ask "how would the mentor help me?" mirror question 2 back

"You said the advice you got was generic and you never found out what was actually wrong. That's the whole job. Your mentor sits with your interview answers, your resume, your LinkedIn, and tells you exactly what to change, before and after, based on what hiring managers in that industry actually screen for and what your strengths are."

Then the mentor, one name, full conviction

"The person for you is [mentor]. [One-line story tied to their field]. Especially strong on [their gap]."

If "does networking actually work?"

"It's how I got my first two offers. Around half of roles go through referrals, not the front door."

If they have big or unrealistic aims, or argue back (e.g. "only top-tier")

Hear them out fully first. Then: "I hear you. Here's the realistic way in..." Never half-disqualify them on the spot.

Never pitch before they ask. Never offer two mentors. Never say "I'm torn."

5

Price

Anchor the session count first, then state the number flat and stop talking.

Say, count before price, always

"For someone in your spot, most people take around 12 to 15 sessions to land a role."

"The trial session is $55, then it's $70 a session after that. Only charged after each one, no lock-in."

Then go silent. Let them react first.

Never say "we charge reasonably." Never walk the number down.

6

Close

Get the commitment on the call.

Say

"Any reason we couldn't get you started with [mentor] next week?"

Then stay silent. Do not fill it with more value.

If "let me think" or "can I tell you next week?"

"How about tomorrow?" Then: "Is this something you're looking to sort now, or still weighing up? What's making you want to wait, the money, the time, or whether it'll actually work?" Then handle the real one.

1 · If it's the money

"12 sessions is about $825 to land a role paying $70k plus. And you only pay per session, as you go." Open the ROI tab and run their numbers.

2 · If it's the time

Use their date from question 4. "You're not close to ready, and that's fine, that's what this is for. But the mentor gives you the answers, you're the one executing them, and that takes reps. Waiting just means more sessions to get to the same place." Then the visa frame: "Plenty of things can wait. With a two-year visa, this one has a hard deadline on it."

3 · If it's whether it'll actually work

If you never ran the test, this is the signal. Run it now (stage 3), then re-close off the back of it: "Based on what I just heard, waiting doesn't put you in a better spot."

If buying signal, "when can I start?"

Stop selling. "Great, I'll send the agreement now, takes 30 seconds, and I'll lock [mentor] in for you."

Logistics

Send the agreement link plus card details. Open a WhatsApp group with the mentor. Set the first session time.

Never cave when they pause. Never joke about scams or trust.
